Before You Write

Common questions.

Everything you need to know before you reach out — answered honestly.

Crete · Wedding Floristry
01 Can we book you from abroad without visiting first? +

Yes — the majority of our couples are international. We work entirely over email and video call. Many couples see their flowers for the first time on their wedding day, and it is always exactly as we planned together.

02 How far in advance do we need to book? +

For peak season (May–October) we recommend 6–12 months in advance. That said, write to us regardless — we always do our best. For smaller arrangements like a bridal bouquet, a few weeks is often enough.

03 Do you work with all venues in Crete? +

Yes. After two decades on the island, we have deep relationships with venues across Neapoli, Agios Nikolaos, Elounda, and Sitia. Tell us your venue and we'll take it from there.

04 What if a flower I love isn't in season? +

We'll tell you honestly. Then we'll find you something equally beautiful that is in season. Crete's local flowers are extraordinary — often the seasonal suggestion becomes the couple's favourite part of the whole arrangement.

05 What is your minimum order? +

There is none. A single bridal bouquet is as welcome as a full venue commission. Every arrangement — regardless of size — receives the same care and intention.
